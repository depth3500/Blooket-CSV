Blooket Answer Key → CSV

Turn a Blooket answer-key PDF into a CSV you can upload with Blooket's CSV Import. Everything runs in your browser; your file is never uploaded anywhere.

  1. In Blooket, open your set → Print. Turn off shuffling and images, turn on "show answers" (correct choice bolded).
  2. Save the answer key as a PDF, then drop it below.
  3. Check the preview, download the CSV, then in Blooket use Create → CSV Import.
